        <li><strong>Desk (Internal) Audit:</strong> Conducted at the tax office, this involves reviewing submitted tax returns and registered data. The taxpayer may be requested to provide specific supporting documents.</li>
        <li><strong>Field Audit:</strong> A tax inspector visits the company's premises to examine physical accounting books, records, and inventory to ensure they align with the submitted tax declarations.</li>
        <li><strong>Sample-Based Audit:</strong> A modern system that relies on automated risk analysis software to randomly select a certain percentage of tax files for examination based on predefined risk criteria.</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>What to Do if You Disagree with the Audit Results? (Tax Appeals)</h3>
      <p>If the audit results (typically issued via Tax Form 19) are inaccurate or rely on arbitrary estimations, Egyptian tax law grants you the right to formally appeal the assessment within 30 days from the date of receipt.</p>
      <p><strong>The Appeal Process:</strong></p>
      <ul>
        <li><strong>Internal Committees:</strong> The first stage of dispute resolution conducted within the tax office. Disputes are often resolved here, and assessments are frequently reduced when strong supporting documentation is presented.</li>
        <li><strong>Tax Appeal Committees:</strong> Independent quasi-judicial committees that review the dispute if an agreement cannot be reached at the Internal Committee stage.</li>
        <li><strong>Administrative Courts:</strong> The final legal recourse if the taxpayer rejects the decision issued by the Tax Appeal Committee.</li>
      </ul>
